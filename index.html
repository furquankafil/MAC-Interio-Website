The MAC INTERIO Approach

Furniture designed around your vision.

MAC INTERIO brings together marble, wood and metal to create furniture that feels architectural yet personal — made around your space, not pulled from a fixed shelf. Little Star, our first showroom, presents these pieces to clients.

Because the company manufactures directly in New Delhi, clients work with the maker: premium materials, factory craftsmanship and a factory-to-client approach that keeps every detail considered — from stone selection to final finish.

  • Direct manufacturing — no dealers, no middlemen
  • 100% customization of size, material, finish & seating
  • Manufacturer pricing with premium materials

Material-Led Making

Three Materials. One Signature.

Every MAC INTERIO piece begins with the material — selected, cut and finished in our own workshop.

No. 01

Marble

Natural character, veining and depth. Each stone top is chosen for its movement and polished to a timeless, light-catching finish.

No. 02

Wood

Warmth, structure and craftsmanship. Turned legs, carved frames and solid tops — joinery built to hold its presence for decades.

No. 03

Metal

Strength, precision and refined finishes — including SS PVD gold where the design calls for a quiet gleam.

Founder · Umar Faruq

The Maker Behind the Brand

A Manufacturer's Perspective on Luxury

Umar Faruq founded MAC INTERIO on a simple conviction — premium furniture becomes more accessible when you control the manufacturing instead of reselling finished products.

Because every piece is made directly, clients get better customization, direct communication with the maker, manufacturer pricing, closer control over materials, and a finish that is personally overseen.
