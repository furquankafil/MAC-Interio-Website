Our Story

Crafted directly by the manufacturer.

MAC INTERIO — MAC for Marble Art and Craft, INTERIO for Interior — operates from its own factory, workshop and showroom in New Delhi.

That single fact shapes everything we make. Instead of choosing from fixed catalogue sizes, clients work with the maker: stone is selected for its veining, wood is joined and finished in-house, and metal bases are fabricated to the design. Little Star is the first showroom face of MAC INTERIO — from stone selection to final finish, every detail is considered, and every piece is designed to belong to your space.

Umar Faruq · Founder

Founder

Umar Faruq

“Luxury should be made around the client — not picked from a shelf.”

Umar Faruq's vision is to make premium furniture more accessible by controlling the manufacturing process rather than simply reselling finished products. Because MAC INTERIO manufactures directly, clients get better customization, direct communication with the maker, manufacturer pricing, closer control over materials, and personal attention to finish.

  • Direct communication — you speak with the maker
  • Manufacturer pricing — no dealer margins
  • Material control — stone, wood and finish, chosen with you
